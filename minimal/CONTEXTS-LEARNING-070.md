# X4 0.1.70: learning completion and activity

Built on the qualified 0.1.69 image. Contexts 0.4.1, Contexts service 0.3.2 and scene-host 0.3.1 replace the three affected modules. Home, native runtime, panel 0.1.16, Lists and all other apps/providers retain the .69 bytes.

Recording shows the component bible's partial ring. After zero, delayed evidence collection has an explicit Finishing capture stage, accepted-window count and animated activity. Saving is shown before verified persistence. Success opens All set automatically. Missing/broken captures terminate at 45 seconds with Retry; save failures preserve the captured data for an explicit retry. Learning uses resident idle/policy inhibition without enabling background Context Detection.

The scripts bind exact source trees, component receipts and the .69 baseline. They validate all 49 ELFs and the complete cohort normally and under sanitizers, rebuild the store deterministically, perform two independent readbacks, check partition capacity, and verify firmware/store pairing and unchanged flash regions. Hardware accuracy remains untested.

The full 16 MiB image flashes at 0x0 and replaces internal settings, Bluetooth bonds and AppData, including trained profiles. SD contents are not part of the image. No device was flashed.
