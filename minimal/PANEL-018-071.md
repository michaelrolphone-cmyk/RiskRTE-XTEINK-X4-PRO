This private test selects upstream x4pro-uc8279-fast 0.1.18, exact source
045b610b82bb6d423215dcd83fd901be9d6f93ed. The driver and its selected source

## Diagnostic limitation

The upstream .18 experiment intentionally leaves the panel powered and refuses
sleep after displaying an image. It omits automatic panel power-off. Higher idle
power is expected; battery impact is not measured. The user explicitly requested
this test while the driver owner prepares a version restoring normal sleep.
This limitation is not a product sleep fix and must not become the baseline.

## Qualification

The Xtensa ELF uses the previously qualified GCC 8.4 O1 profile, validates
relocations/imports/exports and goes through native admission after compaction.
The selected upstream differential/no-POF case and 16 error/probe cases pass
normally and under ASan/UBSan. The historical generic cases expecting older
waveforms/power-off do not describe .18; the runner selects the applicable matrix.

The actual production adapter, Runtime and panel run at 1/8/20/50 ms app polling
intervals with 25 ms of modeled foreground work. Active differential updates,
exact-target endpoint settling, bounded provider slices, continuing touch samples
and 30 seconds without extra refresh pass normally and under sanitizers. The
actual product Light helper survives three refused attempts with pending and
finished settling, restores input/storage custody and permits the next frame.
These are software models, not physical timing, contrast or ghosting measurements.

Build with build_selected_panel_018.py, create_panel_018_071_spec.py and
package_panel_018_071.py. Source selection and inherited publication mappings are
pinned in the adjacent JSON records. The packager verifies clean sources, input
hashes, 49 ELFs, normal/sanitized cohort admission, deterministic SPIFFS with two
independent readbacks, firmware/store pairing and preserved flash regions.

Deliverable: direct full 16 MiB BIN at offset 0x0. Full flashing replaces internal
settings, bonds and AppData; SD contents are outside this image. No physical
device test, flashing, merge or release was performed.
